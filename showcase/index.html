Found along the way

Building these meant reading every product page, the 2026 catalog and about sixty of your blog posts. A few small things turned up that are quick to fix.

  • The caviar quiz has dead endsTwo of its routing rules can never be true, so customers who score 2, 3, 4 or -1 reach the default end screen with no caviar recommended. The Atlas quiz can replace it.
  • Two jar sizes, one priceSmoked American Salmon Caviar lists the 1 oz and the 2 oz jars at the same $25.00.
  • A species line copied from another pageReserve Beluga Sevruga says "Huso huso x Acipenser ruthenus" (the Bester cross). Its description and the catalog say sevruga, Acipenser stellatus.
  • Two Latin names in the 2026 catalogAlbino Sterlet is printed as Acipenser stellatus and Italian White Sturgeon as Acipenser schrenckii. The product pages have them right.
  • Five answers to "how long does it keep?"Unopened shelf life reads 2 to 4 weeks, 4 to 6 weeks, about 4 weeks, 3 to 5 weeks and up to two months across the FAQ and the blog. The shipping cutoff is 1 PM on one page and 2 PM on another.
  • Hickory or bourbon barrel?The smoked trout roe copy credits Pappy Van Winkle barrel staves; the page title says natural hickory smoked.
