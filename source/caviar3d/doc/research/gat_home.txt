Great Food for Great People

Since 1991, Great Atlantic Trading has specialized in sourcing and processing unique seafoods and specialties. We source and import all of our goods without middlemen, expertly grading and packaging only the finest caviar, roes, and specialty foods for use in America’s top kitchens. We work directly with U.S. fishermen and leading sustainable farms at home and abroad to deliver unmatched variety and consistency.

Beyond caviar, our curated pantry includes truffles and truffle products, escargot, premium olive and nut oils, aged vinegars, cured and tinned seafood, smoked fish, squid ink, fresh vanilla beans, spices, and caviar serving ware.
​
Family-run and ethics-driven, our long-standing reputation and enduring partnerships reflect a simple promise: best-in-class products and attentive service.
