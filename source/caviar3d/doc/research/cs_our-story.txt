The Caviar Star Story

How We Got Here

The Caviar Star story is a good one. Founded in Portland, Maine, in the late 1980s by husband-and-wife divers Dana and Denise Leavitt, Great Atlantic began as a true hands-on seafood operation—one built on early mornings, cold waters, and an uncompromising respect for the ocean. The Leavitts harvested scallops and urchins themselves, selling their catch directly on the Portland docks. What started as a modest coastal venture quickly expanded into a global export business, especially throughout Japan, and eventually evolved into Great Atlantic Trading Inc. as their passion for caviar and exceptional roes took center stage.

Great Atlantic Trading Inc. is the proud parent company behind the Caviar Star brand and a trusted name in gourmet caviar and specialty foods for more than three decades. Since our founding in 1991, we’ve been driven by a simple but ambitious mission: to bring the world’s finest delicacies to your table at prices that remain refreshingly accessible. You can learn more about wholesale ordering and partner with our parent company at Great Atlantic Trading Inc.

More than three decades later, that same commitment to craftsmanship defines who we are.

Caviar Star has become a trusted resource for distributors, wholesalers, chefs, and home cooks who seek world-class caviar and specialty ingredients—truffles, aged balsamics, premium vinegars, and beyond—at genuine wholesale value. By sourcing directly from farms and fisheries and partnering with artisanal producers worldwide, we make Michelin-level quality accessible to anyone with a love for fine flavor.

Integrity is the foundation of everything we do. From responsible sourcing to careful handling, we value transparency at every step. Whether you’re a chef pushing culinary boundaries or a curious gourmand discovering caviar for the first time, our goal is the same: to offer exceptional products you can trust.

Our Commitment to Caviar

Caviar is a luxury, but at Caviar Star, it’s a luxury meant to be enjoyed. Our high-capacity facility and deeply trained team allow us to offer top-tier caviar at the fairest possible prices. Every batch, domestic and imported, is personally inspected for quality, freshness, and consistency. We adhere to rigorous government regulations and food-safety protocols, making sure every tin that leaves our facility meets the highest standards.

Today, Caviar Star brings expertly curated caviar to customers across the country, delivering both domestic and imported selections that highlight freshness and flavor. While premium caviar often carries an intimidating price tag, we believe that true luxury should be inviting, not exclusive. Our team works tirelessly to make sure that every tin reflects the integrity of its origin and the high standards our customers have come to expect.
