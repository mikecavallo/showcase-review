# Great Atlantic Trading Inc. / Caviar Star: research dossier

Compiled 2026-10-06. Every source used is saved as text in `doc/research/` (URL on line 1). Images are in
`doc/research/img/` with `sources.csv`. Labels used below:

- **VERIFIED**: confirmed by two independent sources, or by a primary source (the company's own published
  statement about itself, an official record, or a dated document). Company self-statements are marked
  "(company-stated)" so the script can attribute them ("the family says").
- **SINGLE-SOURCE**: one non-primary source, or a company claim that cannot be checked (superlatives,
  firsts, rankings).
- **UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT**: search-engine snippet only, or sources disagree.

Most of what is known about the founders comes from the company's own websites and catalog, and from a
distributor profile (Baldor) whose wording reads as company-supplied. No independent interview, podcast,
news feature or trade-press profile of Dana or Denise Leavitt was found.

## 4. The two "founding dates", resolved

- **Late 1980s**: the diving years in Portland (Great Atlantic Seafood, urchins and scallops, the docks). The
  "35 years" marketing line counts from here.
- **1991**: the logo's ESTD date and "since our founding in 1991": the founding of Great Atlantic Trading.
- **1989**: one undated Caviar Star page says American caviar exports began in 1989. It conflicts with the
  other texts; do not use it in narration.

Recommended narration: "In the late nineteen eighties ... By 1991, Great Atlantic Trading was born."

Pantry beyond caviar (GAT home): truffles, escargot, olive and nut oils, aged vinegars, cured and tinned
seafood, smoked fish, squid ink, vanilla beans, spices, serving ware. 2026 catalog "Smart Guide" lists origin
flags for USA, France, Spain, Italy, Poland, Bulgaria, China, Denmark, Romania, Taiwan, Germany, Norway,
Canada, Turkey (good for a sourcing map).

Packing: "Custom-packed daily to order", jars and tins from 1 oz to 1 kg; "hand-packed to order from large
format original caviar tins" (Baldor); "Personally inspecting each container received, extensively
re-grading, and hand-packing every order daily" (GAT About).

Shipping and green practices: perishables UPS Next Day Air; free shipping over $500; insulated packaging keeps
cold up to 72 hours (shipping page; FAQ says 48 hours); recycled-cotton or compostable plant-based insulation;
UPS Carbon Neutral since 2023; cardboard waste shredded into packing material; plastic and glass hand-washed
and recycled.

Regulation (company-stated, consistent with USFWS/CITES): sturgeon and paddlefish exports need CITES permits
with the lot number on every label; the company says it is inspected yearly and works with USFWS, APHIS,
NOAA and FDA HACCP rules; "thousands of shipments already delivered to overseas customers" (export page).
