OUR STORY Great Atlantic began on the wharves of Portland, Maine in the late 1980’s when husband and wife, Dana and Denise Leavitt, started commercially diving for sea urchin and scallops. After a few years, their small local operation quickly grew into a global business, exporting Uni and evenutaully caviar to Japan. As their passion for caviar evolved, Great Atlantic Trading Inc. was born, becoming a leading supplier of American and imported caviar and roe. Today, from a coastal North Carolina base with their son and daughter, they continue as a proud family-run business with a passion for bringing the finest caviar, truffles, vinegars, and other specialties to American kitchens. Partnerships with artisanal purveyors and sustainable farms paired with volume import consolidation allows us to deliver Michelin-quality products to chefs and culinary enthusiasts at unbeatable prices. 2 At Great Atlantic, we are committed to transparency, sustainability, and integrity. Every product we source undergoes rigorous quality control, ensuring the highest standards in every bite. We pride ourselves on affordability without compromising quality, thanks to our high-volume facility and expert staff. Sustainability is also prioritized through partnering with reputable farms and using green practices in our operations and packaging. We’re dedicated to providing exceptional service and quality products. Join the Great Atlantic family and experience the passion in every bite! GREATATLANTICTRADING.COM
