Taste Influencers & Caviar Brand Ambassadors

Do you have a passion for exceptional food, beautiful entertaining, and creating inspiring culinary content? Caviar Star is always looking to partner with talented creators who share our appreciation for luxury ingredients and memorable dining experiences.

Our Ideal Caviar Brand Ambassadors

We love working with culinary personalities, hospitality professionals, and food content creators! Whether you're a chef, recipe developer, gourmet food photographer, hospitality professional, or lifestyle creator, we'd love to hear from you if your content reflects the quality and craftsmanship behind our products.

Our ideal ambassadors and content partners have:

An elevated aesthetic with high-quality food, entertaining, or lifestyle photography and video

An engaged audience of food lovers, home entertainers, chefs, luxury consumers, or thoughtful gift buyers

A genuine appreciation for premium ingredients and fine dining

A professional, brand-safe online presence

Strong audience engagement, regardless of follower count

We're especially excited to connect with creators who have relationships within the culinary or hospitality industry and can help showcase our produts through authentic restaurant, chef, or event collaborations.

Luxury and Gourmet Food Influencers

Before moving forward with a partnership, our team evaluates audience demographics and engagement, content quality and photography style first and foremost. We also consider previous brand collabs, content usage rights, and exclusivity requirements before discussing compensation.

Partnership Compensation Models

Every collaboration is tailored to the creator and campaign, but all partnerships should include content deliverables approved for use across Caviar Star marketing channels.

Free-Product Only

You may receive complimentary Caviar Star products in exchange for agreed-upon content, such as original photography, video, recipes, social posts, or stories. These partnerships are ideal for creators who genuinely enjoy fine food and can produce high-quality content that authentically showcases the Caviar Star experience.

Affiliate Commission

Affiliate partnerships give creators the opportunity to earn commission on qualifying purchases you generate for Caviar Star. This model is well suited to accounts with an engaged audience interested in caviar, gourmet food, entertaining, luxury dining, and gifting. This option also provides the opportunity to build an ongoing relationship based on performance.

Paid Partnerships

We consider paid collaborations with select creators whose audience, content quality, reach, and creative approach strongly align with our brand and campaign goals. Paid partnerships are evaluated individually and may include specific content deliverables, usage rights, campaign timelines, and exclusivity requirements.

FAQs
Q: Do I need a minimum number of followers?
A: No. While we generally prefer to work with creators who have a large following, we're equally interested in niche creators with highly engaged communities and exceptional content.

Q: Do you offer paid partnerships?
A: Yes. Paid partnerships may be available for select creators and brand ambassadors. We carefully evaluate each opportunity to ensure the partnership is a strong fit for both Caviar Star and the creator, considering audience relevance, engagement, content quality, proposed deliverables, and overall brand alignment.

Q: How long does the application review process take?
A: You should typically allow approximately two weeks for our team to evaluate submissions and contact creators who may be a good fit for upcoming collaborations.

Applications for consideration should be submitted by October 31. Due to the busy holiday season, applications are not reviewed during November and December. We resume reviewing prospective partners in January each year.
