There are a lot of ways to serve caviar, but Chef James London’s Caviar Sammich at Chubby Fish might be one of our favorites.

The celebrated Charleston seafood restaurant is known for taking exceptional ingredients and serving them in ways that feel fun rather than fussy, and the Caviar Sammich is a perfect example: warm, buttery bread, cool crème fraîche and a generous layer of caviar.

We’ve been lucky enough to work with Chef James and the Chubby Fish team, and this now-famous sandwich has inspired more than a few cravings around here. So we put together a version you can make at home using our VIP Imperial Kaluga Hybrid Caviar.

It’s simple, decadent and proof that sometimes the best way to eat really good caviar is between two pieces of buttered bread.

Why Kaluga Hybrid Works So Well

For a caviar sandwich, you want something with enough flavor and texture to stand up to butter and crème fraîche without becoming overly salty.

VIP Imperial Kaluga Hybrid has large, firm pearls with a creamy texture, buttery flavor and gentle salinity. It’s rich without overpowering the other ingredients, which makes it especially well suited for generous spoonfuls and simple preparations like this one.

Chubby Fish–Inspired Caviar Sammich

Ingredients

Small potato rolls

Softened butter

Crème fraîche

Fresh chives, finely chopped

Fresh Lemon

Freshly cracked black pepper

Flaky sea salt, if needed

1 (50-gram) tin of Caviar Star VIP Imperial Kaluga Hybrid Caviar

Plan on approximately 15–20 grams of caviar per sandwich.

Instructions

1. Toast the rolls

Slice the potato rolls and lightly butter the cut sides. Toast in a skillet over medium heat until deeply golden while keeping the outer parts soft and pillowy.

2. Make the crème fraîche

Lightly whip the crème fraîche, then fold in chopped chives, a small squeeze of fresh lemon juice and tiny bit of freshly cracked black pepper.

We don't find the need to add more salt—the caviar will bring plenty of salinity on its own. But taste first then add some, if you please.

3. Build the sammich

Spoon a generous layer of caviar onto one side of the warm, toasted roll. Add a dollop of the crème fraîche mixture and finish with a little freshly grated lemon zest.

4. Finish and serve

Close the sandwich and lightly brush the top with melted butter.

Serve immediately while the bread is still warm and the crème fraîche and caviar are cool.

That contrast is part of what makes it so good.

What to Serve With a Caviar Sandwich

You don't need much.

Like most caviar as a service or caviar snacks, this sandwich is rich and satisfying, typically best enjoyed at the beginning of a meal as an appetizer, as a special treat on date night, or as the ultimate finger sandwich at brunch.

At home if you simply pair a couple of these bad boys with a lemony salad, a handful of kettle chips--you have a delicious light and decadent meal. For drinks, a crisp sparkling wine, rose, or dry vinho verde pairs naturally with the buttery, briny flavors of the caviar.

Bringing Restaurant Luxury Home

Part of what makes the Chubby Fish caviar sandwich so memorable is the balance between comfort and luxury. Familiar ingredients like bread, butter, and cream create a rich, approachable base, while the caviar adds something special without making the dish feel overly fussy.

Kaluga Hybrid is especially well suited for this style of sandwich, with large pearls, a creamy texture, and a mild, buttery flavor that complements rather than overwhelms the other ingredients.

It’s the kind of dish that works just as well for a weekend brunch or date night as it does for a casual celebration at home—and a good reminder that enjoying caviar doesn’t always have to be a formal occasion.
