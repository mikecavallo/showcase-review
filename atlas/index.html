The pearl scale

Every roe, drawn to size, in its own colour

Each pearl is one product, drawn at nine times life size in the colour sampled from the shop's own photograph, and placed by price per gram against grain size. Where the shop gives no size, the pearl uses the species' published egg size. The red roes are the biggest pearls and the cheapest; sturgeon are smaller and dearer, because a sturgeon takes years to make her first eggs.
