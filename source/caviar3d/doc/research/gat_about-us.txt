Our​ Global Search for The World's Finest

Founded in Portland, Maine in the late 1980s by husband-and-wife divers Dana and Denise Leavitt, Great Atlantic Seafood began as a small hands-on operation—harvesting scallops and urchins and selling them on the Portland docks. This small venture quickly grew into a global trading company, with majority of products being sent to Japan, evolving into Great Atlantic Trading Inc. as the Leavitts’ passion for caviar and fine roes took center stage.

For over three and a half decades, we’ve been a trusted source for distributors, wholesalers, chefs, and home cooks seeking exceptional caviar and specialty ingredients—truffles, aged balsamics, and more—at true wholesale value. By buying directly from farms and fisheries and importing at scale, we keep Michelin-caliber quality within reach.
At Caviar Star, integrity guides everything we do. We source meticulously, handle expertly, and prioritize transparency and sustainability—whether you’re a chef pushing creative boundaries or a curious gourmand discovering something new.

Supporting local and sustainable practices is important to us and we believe that it produces a superior product for our customers. In most cases, we take the time to visit our suppliers farms and production centers to ensure their standards of practice meets ours, in regards to aspects health, animal welfare, environmental impact, and more. All of our wild-caught and farmed roes are regulated in accordance with FDA and Fish & Wildlife guidelines, ensuring the roe is legally sourced and sustainable. 

Quality caviar is always an expensive product. We strive to make it as affordable as possible while still upholding the integrity of the specific species, farm, and industry. Personally inspecting each container received, extensively re-grading, and hand-packing every order daily helps ensure our customers get only the very best quality products. 

The finest chefs, caterers, retail customers and gourmet aficionados around the country appreciate these high-quality, rare products and excellent customer care.

We invite you to join them and see for yourself, and look forward to serving you!

- The Leavitt Family
